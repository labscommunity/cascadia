=== K2.6 KV SDPA: bf16 upconvert cost decomposition ===
Single-head, scalar Rust. Cache lives in L1/L2 at small past_seq_len.
Goal: quantify the inline `f32::from_bits((u16 as u32) << 16)` upconvert cost
in iter 032's SDPA loop. Decision rule in source-file doc comment.

  bf16 inline            1.081 us/iter    9.48 GB/s read   (sink=0)
  bf16 split             1.363 us/iter    7.51 GB/s read   (sink=0)
  bf16 upcvt only        0.211 us/iter   48.61 GB/s read   (sink=0)
  f32 dot only           0.991 us/iter   20.66 GB/s read   (sink=0)
  ---
  upconvert cost (inline - f32 dot only):   0.089 us  (  8.3% of bf16 inline)
  upconvert cost (isolated upcvt only):     0.211 us  ( 19.5% of bf16 inline)
  split-pass slowdown vs inline:           -26.13% (negative = split is slower)
  f32 (raw, no cvt):                        0.993 us
