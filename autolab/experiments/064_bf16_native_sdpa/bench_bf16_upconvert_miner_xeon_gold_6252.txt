=== K2.6 KV SDPA: bf16 upconvert cost decomposition ===
Single-head, scalar Rust. Cache lives in L1/L2 at small past_seq_len.
Goal: quantify the inline `f32::from_bits((u16 as u32) << 16)` upconvert cost
in iter 032's SDPA loop. Decision rule in source-file doc comment.

=== past_seq_len = 16 (QK_HEAD_DIM=192, V_HEAD_DIM=128) ===
  f32 SDPA               5.770 us/iter    3.55 GB/s read   (sink=0)
  bf16 inline            5.992 us/iter    1.71 GB/s read   (sink=0)
  bf16 split             6.706 us/iter    1.53 GB/s read   (sink=0)
  bf16 upcvt only        0.704 us/iter   14.55 GB/s read   (sink=0)
  f32 dot only           5.939 us/iter    3.45 GB/s read   (sink=0)
  ---
  upconvert cost (inline - f32 dot only):   0.053 us  (  0.9% of bf16 inline)
  upconvert cost (isolated upcvt only):     0.704 us  ( 11.7% of bf16 inline)
  split-pass slowdown vs inline:           -11.91% (negative = split is slower)
  f32 (raw, no cvt):                        5.770 us
